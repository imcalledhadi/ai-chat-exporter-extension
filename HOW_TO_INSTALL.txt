================================================================================
                    AI CHAT EXPORTER — INSTALLATION GUIDE
================================================================================

This guide explains how to install and use this extension in Google Chrome,
Brave, Microsoft Edge, Opera, or any Chromium-based web browser.

No technical skills required! It takes less than 1 minute.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: OPEN EXTENSIONS IN YOUR BROWSER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Choose your browser and open the extensions page:

• Google Chrome:
  Type  chrome://extensions  in your address bar and press Enter.

• Brave Browser:
  Type  brave://extensions   in your address bar and press Enter.

• Microsoft Edge:
  Type  edge://extensions    in your address bar and press Enter.

• Opera / Opera GX:
  Type  opera://extensions   in your address bar and press Enter.

(Alternatively: Click the puzzle piece icon (🧩) at the top-right of your
 browser and select "Manage Extensions".)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: TURN ON "DEVELOPER MODE"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Look at the top-right corner of the Extensions page.
You will see a toggle switch labeled:

    [ Developer mode ]  <--- CLICK TO TURN ON (Switch should turn blue)

(In Microsoft Edge, this toggle is located on the left sidebar: "Developer mode")


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: LOAD THE EXTENSION FOLDER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Look at the top-left corner of the page.
2. Click the button that says:

    [ Load unpacked ]

3. A folder selection window will pop up.
4. Browse to and select THIS folder (the one containing manifest.json).
   Example:
   D:\Extension
5. Click "Select Folder".

The extension "AI Chat Exporter" will instantly appear in your list!


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: PIN TO YOUR TOOLBAR (RECOMMENDED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To make the extension easily accessible anytime:

1. Click the puzzle icon (🧩) in the top-right corner of your browser.
2. Find "AI Chat Exporter" in the list.
3. Click the pin icon (📌) next to it.
4. The extension icon will now stay visible on your browser toolbar.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5: HOW TO USE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open any supported AI chatbot conversation:
   • Claude        (claude.ai)
   • ChatGPT       (chatgpt.com)
   • Google Gemini (gemini.google.com)
   • xAI Grok      (grok.com or x.com)
   • DeepSeek      (chat.deepseek.com)
   • Meta AI       (meta.ai)
   • Perplexity    (perplexity.ai)
   • Copilot       (copilot.microsoft.com)

2. Features you can use inside the chat:
   • DRAGGABLE EXPORT BUTTON:
     A floating purple "Export" button appears on the screen.
     Click and drag it anywhere on your screen to place it wherever you want.
     Click it to open an instant 1-click export menu.

   • PER-RESPONSE EXPORT:
     Under every AI response, click the small download icon to export just
     that specific answer (PDF, Word, Markdown, HTML, TXT, JSON).

   • LIMIT TRACKER & HANDOFF:
     Below the chat input box, see your token usage and click
     "Continue In..." to transfer the conversation to another AI bot with 1 click.

   • EXTENSION POPUP:
     Click the extension icon in your browser toolbar to export the full chat,
     select formats, adjust settings, or customize export defaults.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO UPDATE AFTER CODE CHANGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Whenever new files or updates are added to this folder:
1. Go back to  chrome://extensions
2. Find "AI Chat Exporter".
3. Click the circular reload icon (🔄).
4. Refresh your open AI chat tabs in your browser.


================================================================================
Done! Enjoy exporting your AI conversations cleanly and easily.
